# Aptitude test · Set 1

Candidates report about **28 questions in 30 minutes** covering arithmetic, verbal and
non-verbal reasoning. That is about 64 seconds per question, so set a timer and don't get stuck.

Write your answers in `aptitude-set-1-my-answers.md` (one line per question, e.g. `1. 42`),
then compare them with `aptitude-set-1-answers.md`. Only open the answers when you're done.

## Numerical (1–14)

1. Next number: 2, 6, 12, 20, 30, ?
2. Next number: 3, 5, 9, 17, 33, ?
3. Next number: 1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, ?
4. Next number: 81, 27, 9, 3, ?
5. A desk costs €320. It gets a 15% discount, then 21% VAT is added. Final price?
6. A price rises by 20% and then falls by 20%. What is the net change in percent?
7. 3 developers fix 12 bugs in 4 hours. At the same rate, how many bugs do 5 developers fix in 6 hours?
8. A train leaves at 08:40 and travels 210 km at 84 km/h. When does it arrive?
9. The ratio of developers to testers is 5:2. There are 63 people in total. How many testers?
10. The average of 5 numbers is 18. One number is removed and the average of the other 4 is 16. Which number was removed?
11. An invoice of €1,200 is paid in 3 instalments in the ratio 1:2:3. How large is the biggest instalment?
12. 40% of A equals 25% of B. If A = 50, what is B?
13. Pipe X fills a tank in 6 hours, pipe Y in 3 hours. How long do they take together?
14. How many minutes are in 3.5% of a day?

## Verbal and logical (15–24)

15. *Module* is to *ERP* as *plugin* is to: (a) code (b) browser (c) install (d) update
16. Odd one out: PostgreSQL, MySQL, SQLite, Python, MariaDB
17. All developers are engineers. Some engineers are managers. Which statement is certainly true?
    (a) Some developers are managers (b) All engineers are developers
    (c) We cannot conclude whether any developer is a manager (d) No developer is a manager
18. "If it rains, the match is cancelled." The match was not cancelled. So:
    (a) It rained (b) It did not rain (c) Cannot tell
19. Sara is taller than Omar. Omar is taller than Lea. Lea is taller than Karim. Who is the second shortest?
20. "Only confirmed orders are invoiced." Order X is invoiced. Is "Order X is confirmed" true, false, or impossible to tell?
21. Which word can be made from the letters of DEVELOPMENT (each letter used at most as often as it appears)?
    (a) PLANET (b) MODEST (c) TEMPLE (d) VELVET
22. Closest meaning of "deprecated" in software:
    (a) discouraged and planned for removal (b) encrypted (c) duplicated (d) compiled
23. If CODE is written DPEF, how is SALE written?
24. 1 January 2026 is a Thursday. What day is 1 March 2026?

## Non-verbal (25–28)

25. Next in the sequence: ▲ · ■ · ▲▲ · ■■ · ▲▲▲ · ?
26. Find the missing value:
    ```
    2   4   8
    3   9   27
    4   16  ?
    ```
27. What is the smaller angle between the hour and minute hands at 3:30?
28. An arrow points right (→). It is rotated 90° clockwise three times. Where does it point?
